Introduction

Zen Stack is a casual mobile game. This policy explains how information may be handled when you play the game.

Information stored on your device

Zen Stack does not require an account or operate its own game server. Game progress and high scores are stored locally on your device.

Advertising

Zen Stack uses Google AdMob to display rewarded advertisements. AdMob may process device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information, depending on the device, region, consent choices, and SDK configuration.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses information from apps: Google partner sites policy

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored game data can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game. Data handled by Google AdMob is subject to Google's policies.

Children's privacy

Advertising and age-related settings are configured in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game or its services change.
