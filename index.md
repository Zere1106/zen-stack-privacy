# Privacy Policy — Zen Stack

**Last updated:** October 9, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Zen Stack (“the Game”) is a casual mobile game developed by an independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you play Zen Stack.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

Zen Stack does not require an account and does not operate its own game server. Game progress and high scores are stored locally on your device.

The developer does not receive locally stored game progress or high scores through a game backend.

## 3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Zen Stack uses Google AdMob to display rewarded advertisements. Although the Game can be played offline in its core gameplay, an internet connection is required for advertisements to load.

Google AdMob may collect and process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information to provide advertisements, measure performance, and help prevent fraud. The information processed may depend on your device, region, consent choices, and the SDK configuration.

Google processes information under its own privacy policies and terms.

For more information, please visit:

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 4. Data Sharing

The developer does not operate a backend to collect or share your game progress. However, information may be transmitted to Google and its advertising services through the AdMob SDK as described above.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and high scores stored locally can generally be removed by clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling the Game, subject to your device's operating system behavior.

Information handled by Google AdMob is subject to Google's retention and deletion practices.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Zen Stack is not specifically intended to collect personal information from children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from players through an account or game server.

Advertising and age-related settings must be configured in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play policies.

## 7. Security

Zen Stack does not maintain a developer-operated server containing player profiles or game progress. Data handled by Google AdMob is subject to Google's applicable security practices.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Game's features, advertising SDKs, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be made available at the published policy URL.
